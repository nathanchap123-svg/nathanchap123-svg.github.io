Our Services

Coops Poop Scoop provides pet waste removal and turf cleaning services to help dog owners keep their yards cleaner and easier to enjoy.

Pet Waste Removal

Pet waste can build up quickly, especially in homes with multiple dogs. Coops Poop Scoop removes pet waste from your yard so you can spend less time cleaning and more time enjoying your outdoor space.

  • Routine pet waste removal
  • One-time yard cleanups
  • Service for homes with one or multiple dogs
  • Convenient and dependable service

Turf Cleaning

Artificial turf can hold odors even after pet waste has been removed. Turf cleaning helps keep outdoor areas cleaner and more comfortable for pets, families and guests.

  • Cleaning for artificial turf
  • Removal of pet waste from turf areas
  • Helps reduce unwanted pet odors
  • Helps maintain a cleaner outdoor space
